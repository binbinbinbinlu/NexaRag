# Nexa Demo employee handbook

FICTIONAL TEST DATA ONLY. These are invented policies for retrieval testing,
not the user's company policies. Effective date: 2026-01-01.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees at Nexa Demo receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Submit requests through the People Portal at least 10 working days before the
first day of leave. A line manager must approve the request before travel is booked.
Unused leave carryover is not specified in this handbook.

## Working location

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams meet in the office
on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Exceptions require written approval from a line manager.

## Onboarding

The new-starter welcome session takes place every Tuesday at 10:00 UTC and lasts
45 minutes. A People Operations coordinator sends the invitation. New starters
should finish their security training within their first five working days.
