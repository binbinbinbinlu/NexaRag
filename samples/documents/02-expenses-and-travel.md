# Nexa Demo expenses and travel

FICTIONAL TEST DATA ONLY. These are invented policies for retrieval testing,
not the user's company policies. Effective date: 2026-01-01.

## Claims

Submit expense claims in the Expense Portal within 30 calendar days of purchase.
Attach an itemized receipt to every claim. Finance reviews complete claims within
five working days. Review does not guarantee that payment occurs in that period.

## Travel

Get written line-manager approval before booking business travel. Standard rail
class and economy flights are reimbursable. The hotel reimbursement ceiling is
EUR 160 per night, excluding local tourist taxes. Higher hotel costs require
Finance approval before booking.

## Meals and equipment

Meals during approved overnight business travel are reimbursable up to EUR 45
per day. Alcohol is excluded. Home-office equipment has a one-time allowance of
EUR 300 per employee, with line-manager approval before purchase. The documents
do not specify any recurring annual home-office allowance.
